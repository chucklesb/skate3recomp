## Notable Changes

### Fixes
- **Hair looked too bright and flat in sun, and lost its colour in shade.**
  The native renderer's hair shader now follows the game's own more
  closely:
  - the light falls off fully on the side of the head away from the sun;
  - the hair's normal map is used, which adds strand detail;
  - the shiny highlight runs along each strand's own direction, which the
    native renderer now carries through the skater's animation (before, it
    guessed the direction from the screen).

  Dyed hair keeps its colour in sun, and the highlight sits on the strands
  as it should.

### Scenario harness and tooling
- **Hair A/B switch:** `skate3_native_render_scene_hair_debug` turns single
  parts of the hair shading off or on (highlight, shadow, normal map, and
  others; the bits are listed in the setting's description).
- **Hair scenarios:** `hair_shade.toml`, `hair_shade_stills.toml`,
  `hair_shade_terms.toml`, `hair_shade_terms2.toml` and `hair_open_sun.toml`
  compare the native and emulated hair in sun and in shade, using a saved
  reel.
