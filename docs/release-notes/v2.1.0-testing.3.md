## Notable Changes

### Fixes
- **A shadow flashed across the skater's shirt for one frame (KI-124).**
  On the Odin 2, a dark band sometimes appeared on the back or sleeves of
  the skater's shirt for a single frame, most visibly with the sun behind
  the skater. The shirt cast its shadow from a slightly different cloth
  shape than the one drawn on screen. It now casts with the shape you see.
  `skate3_native_render_scene_shadow_ropa_drawn_shape` turns the fix off for
  comparison.
- **The skater's hair looked see-through (KI-126).** Against the sky or
  distant trees, the outer layer of the skater's hair turned into a blue
  veil, with the trees behind it showing through. It was the sun haze effect
  treating the hair as if it were as far away as the background. The hair
  is solid again. `skate3_native_render_scene_hair_depth` turns the fix off
  for comparison.

### Scenario harness and tooling
- **Slow-motion reels:** `play_reel` takes `speed` (above 0, up to 4; for
  example `speed = 0.25`), for recording or stepping through a moment.
- **Character debug views:** `skate3_native_render_scene_char_debug` shows
  the skater's shadow term, sun angle, or CSM shadow (1 to 4), a solid colour
  per character type (5), or the normal shading with transparency turned off
  (6).
- **Cloth shadow trace:** with `skate3_native_render_scene_char_track` on,
  the log reports each frame how far the old shirt shadow would have been
  from the drawn shirt.
- New test scenarios for KI-124 and KI-126 (`scenarios/ki124_*.toml`,
  `scenarios/ki126_*.toml`).

### Known issues added
- Skate reel playback runs slower and less evenly than gameplay (KI-123).
- The skater can come apart for a frame or two right after a replay jumps
  to another time (KI-125).
