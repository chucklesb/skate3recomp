## Notable Changes

### Game and application
- **30 FPS framerate cap.** Settings → Display → Framerate Cap now offers
  30 FPS (after Auto). A steady 30 is the right choice on handhelds that
  can't hold 60, such as the Odin 2; before, it took a hand-edited config.
- **Testing builds keep their debug info.** This testing build is
  RelWithDebInfo (optimized, debug info kept, ~120 MB), so a crash report from
  your device maps straight to source lines.

### Fixes
- **Body parts flying off the skater (KI-117).** While skating at moderate
  speed, especially when setting up a flip trick (right stick held down), a
  foot, arm or other body piece was sometimes drawn behind the skater, up to
  metres away, then snapped back. It happened constantly at 30 fps. Fixed:
  the pieces now stay on the body.
- **Skater jitter at speed (KI-120).** The skater looked jittery against the
  background, as if it fell behind and caught up again and again, and it got
  worse the faster you went (most visible at 30 fps). The skater and the
  camera were being timed with two different clocks. Fixed: at 8+ m/s the
  skater is now as steady on screen as the game's own frames (on-screen
  jitter down from 6.5 px to 1.3 px per frame typical, 142 px to 8 px worst).
  Traffic and pedestrians are unaffected.
- **Enhanced Shadows put sunny places in shade (KI-119).** With Enhanced
  Shadows on, open areas that should be sunlit were drawn in shadow and the
  skater lost their shadow. The level shadows were cast from a sun about 15°
  too low; they now match the game's baked lighting. One spot still differs:
  under the large overhead structure at the Core Plaza spawn.

### Scenario harness and tooling
The scenario harness ships in every build but does nothing unless it is
switched on from the command line (`--skate3_harness=true` or
`--skate3_harness_scenario=<file>`). It lets testers and developers reproduce
and measure problems:
- **Scripted runs** (`.toml` scenarios): controller input, waits,
  screenshots, game-state readouts and pass/fail checks, with a run folder
  per run (result, events, state, log, screenshots).
- **Live mode:** send commands to a running game, and record your own
  controller input to replay later.
- **Teleport** to named spots across the map.
- **Saved skate.Reel playback** (`play_reel`): plays a saved replay from the
  start or its marker at 1×, with no UI; holds exact frames for screenshots,
  under several settings or on both renderers. Reels can be picked by a
  stable id instead of their position in My Replays.
- **Crash capture:** the faulting address and symbolized stack frames, plus
  a minidump on Windows.
- **Image diffs** between runs, renderers and devices.
- **Performance benchmark** (`perf_reel`): CPU time per renderer stage and
  GPU time per rendering pass, with run-to-run comparison.
- **Diagnostics:** `skate3_native_render_scene_char_track` logs the skater's
  pose timing per frame; Vulkan validation and RenderDoc capture support for
  the native renderer.

See [docs/scenario-harness.md](https://github.com/chucklesb/skate3recomp/blob/linux-aarch64-cross/docs/scenario-harness.md)
for the full reference, and
[docs/known-issues.md](https://github.com/chucklesb/skate3recomp/blob/linux-aarch64-cross/docs/known-issues.md)
for the issues above and the ones still open.
