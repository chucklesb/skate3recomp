## Notable Changes

Everything since v2.1.0-aarch64.2, including what the testing pre-releases
v2.1.0-testing.1 to .7 tried out.

### Game and application
- **30 FPS framerate cap.** Settings → Display → Framerate Cap now offers
  30 FPS, after Auto. A steady 30 is the right choice on handhelds that can't
  hold 60, such as the Odin 2.
- **Faster menus.** Screens that show skater portraits used to let the
  game's own (emulated) renderer redraw the whole world behind the menu.
  - Pause > Edit Skaters, on the Odin 2 uncapped: 29 → 52 fps, worst frame
    100 → 26 ms.
  - Create-a-Skater: 55 → 85 fps.
- **Faster replays (KI-123).** Watching or editing a replay could drop into
  the teens at a 30 fps cap, with bad frame pacing. The replay editor no
  longer runs the emulated renderer for a skater view nobody sees.
  Uncapped replays on the Odin 2 run 4-6 fps faster, with worst frames
  dropping from 44-71 ms to 32-40 ms.
- **Less visible tiling on canal water.** The canals' ripple texture no
  longer repeats in a visible grid
  (`skate3_native_render_scene_water_detile`, 0 for the original look).
- **Lens effect setting:** `skate3_native_render_scene_screen_fx` (on by
  default) turns the camera's fisheye and vignette off, which you mainly see
  in skate reel replays.
- **Sharper photos and reel thumbnails** on the Odin 2.

### Fixes
- **Body parts flew off the skater (KI-117).** At moderate speed, especially
  when setting up a flip trick, a foot or arm was sometimes drawn metres
  behind the skater. It now stays on the body.
- **The skater jittered at speed (KI-120).** The skater and the camera were
  timed with different clocks. At 8+ m/s the skater is now as steady as in
  the game's own frames.
- **Enhanced Shadows put sunny places in shade (KI-119).** The level
  shadows now match the game's baked lighting. One spot under the Core Plaza
  overhead structure still differs.
- **Canal water reflected orange-brown and looked flat (KI-122).** The
  canals now use a port of the game's own water shader: murky water with
  blue glints, shadows from bridges and the skater, and blue sky reflections.
- **A shadow flashed across the skater's shirt for one frame (KI-124).**
- **The skater's hair looked see-through (KI-126)** against the sky or
  distant trees. It is solid again.
- **Hair looked too bright and flat in sun, and lost its colour in shade.**
  It is now shaded like the game's: it uses the normal map, the light falls
  off away from the sun, and the highlight runs along the strands.
- **The team screen kept showing the old skater photo after an edit until
  a restart.** It now shows the new photo right away.
- **Photo challenges showed no photo** on the final showcase screen.
- **Saved reels got a black thumbnail**, or one from another replay.

### Scenario harness and tooling
The scenario harness ships in every build, but does nothing unless it is
switched on from the command line (`--skate3_harness=true` or
`--skate3_harness_scenario=<file>`). Since aarch64.2 it has gained:
- **Scenarios:** scripted input, screenshots, state readouts and pass/fail
  checks; live mode; recording and replaying controller input; teleports to
  named spots or coordinates.
- **`play_reel`:** play a saved skate reel at 1× or in slow motion, with
  exact-frame stills under several settings, and RenderDoc captures.
- **Crash capture, image diffs, a performance benchmark** with per-pass GPU
  times, and **Vulkan validation and RenderDoc** support for the native
  renderer.
- **An emulated-pass census:** `native_render_pass_census` and
  `census_report.py` show which of the game's own draws still run under the
  native renderer.
- **Helper tools** in `tools/harness/`, the newest being `shots.py classify`
  (which known state each screenshot shows) and `gen_cas_portrait.py --watch`.
- **Builds:** the aarch64 build no longer recompiles the whole game every
  time (about 24 s instead of about 12 minutes after a small change).

### Documentation
